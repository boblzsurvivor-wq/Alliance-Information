# Server 337 Alliance Agreement

*This agreement establishes the principles, governance, and cooperation framework for participating alliances on Server 337.*

## 1. Cooperation During State Events and Migration

All alliances participating in this agreement will cooperate during state events, including but not limited to SVS, Glory War, and other server-wide activities.

Participating alliances also recognise that migration is essential to the long-term success and stability of the server. Alliances are expected to work together in good faith to support fair, transparent, and impartial migration processes that benefit the server as a whole.

Alliances are expected to actively participate and support one another whenever possible in pursuit of shared server objectives.

## 2. Representation and Decision-Making

Each participating alliance shall be represented by three (3) authorised members of its leadership team.

The Leadership Council shall consist of these representatives.

An alliance may replace any of its representatives at any time by notifying the Leadership Council.

All proposals, discussions, and votes conducted under this agreement shall take place within the designated Leadership Chat.

Leadership representatives are responsible for gathering feedback and understanding the views of their alliance members before participating in votes.

## 3. Proposals and Voting

Leadership representatives may propose changes, initiatives, or new ideas at any time and may initiate a vote on such matters.

Votes shall remain open for a minimum of 24 hours unless all representatives have voted sooner or the Leadership Council agrees that an urgent decision is required.

Representatives may vote in favour, against, or abstain. Abstentions shall not count toward either side of a vote.

Decisions will be determined by a simple majority of votes cast by participating representatives and accepted once finalized.

In the event of a tie, the proposal shall fail.

## 4. Server Representative

### Selection

A designated representative will be selected to serve as the official public representative of Server 337.

Each alliance may nominate one candidate. The Leadership Council will then vote to select the representative.

### Responsibilities

The Server Representative shall:

* Serve as the primary point of contact for external communications during SVS, EB, GW, migration discussions, and other inter-server matters.
* Facilitate migration discussions and opportunities in a fair, transparent, and impartial manner for the benefit of the server as a whole.
* Represent the interests of all participating alliances equally.
* Foster cooperation among alliances and work to minimize conflicts.
* Act in accordance with decisions approved through the voting process defined in this agreement.

### Removal

The Server Representative may resign at any time.

The Server Representative may also be removed by a vote conducted in accordance with the voting process defined in this agreement.

A replacement representative may be selected through the same voting process.

## 5. Leadership Conduct

Leadership communication channels are intended for constructive discussion, strategic planning, and collaborative problem-solving.

All participants are expected to conduct themselves respectfully and professionally. Harassment, personal attacks, abusive language, disruptive behaviour, or other forms of disrespectful conduct will not be tolerated.

## 6. Communication with Alliance Members

Leaders are responsible for ensuring that significant decisions, strategic plans, and voting outcomes are communicated to their alliance members accurately and transparently.

Alliance members play a vital role in the success of the server and should be kept appropriately informed of decisions that affect the wider community.

## 7. Professionalism and Cooperation

Personal grievances, rivalries, or past conflicts must not influence leadership decisions or interfere with the fair execution of leadership responsibilities.

While cooperation and mutual support are expected among participating alliances, circumstances may occasionally prevent assistance from being provided. In such situations, leaders and members are expected to remain respectful and understanding.

## 8. Reconsideration of Decisions

Any alliance may request a re-vote on a previously approved decision after seven (7) days, allowing time for members to assess the impact of the original decision.

## 9. Dispute Resolution

All disputes shall be addressed at the lowest appropriate level before escalation:

1. Player-to-Player
2. R4-to-R4
3. R5-to-R5
4. Leadership Council Review

Only disputes that cannot be resolved at a lower level may be escalated.

Where a dispute requires a formal decision, the voting procedure defined in this agreement shall apply.

## 10. Non-Compliance

Concerns regarding repeated or significant violations of this agreement may be raised with the Leadership Council for review.

The Leadership Council shall discuss the matter with the alliance or individual concerned and seek a resolution through communication and cooperation.

Where a resolution cannot be reached, the Leadership Council may conduct a vote on appropriate action in accordance with the voting procedure defined in this agreement.

Any action taken under this section shall be proportionate to the nature and severity of the violation.

## 11. Amendments

This agreement may be amended through the voting process defined in this agreement.

Any approved amendment shall take effect immediately unless otherwise specified.
